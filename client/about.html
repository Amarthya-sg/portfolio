An AI / DS engineer who graduated in 2026 with a B.E. in Artificial Intelligence & Data Science from Bearys Institute of Technology, Mangalore. My work sits at the intersection of applied AI and real infrastructure: agent orchestration, retrieval pipelines, and the serverless plumbing that keeps them running in production.

I like problems that involve wiring several small, opinionated systems together until they behave like one coherent thing — six-agent pipelines that have to agree with each other, retrieval systems that need to be right and fast, chatbots that have to hand off cleanly instead of falling apart at the seams. I've picked this up across two internships and a stack of self-directed projects spanning network security, healthcare ML, and blockchain, and I like that the underlying skills transfer freely between them.

Currently job-hunting for AI / DS Engineer, Data Analyst, and Python Developer roles.

Outside of work, I run Fedora on bare metal, rice my own desktop, write GNOME extensions to fix hardware quirks my laptop manufacturer didn't bother to, and occasionally build things for myself — a wireless gamepad system, a personal mpv fork with a battery-status bridge, a multi-agent game just to see if agents could pull off a convincing lie. When I'm not building something I'm usually chasing down whatever hardware bug Fedora and my laptop have decided to disagree about that week.
